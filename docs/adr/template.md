**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision? The constraints, the pressure, what was true at the time. Written so
it still makes sense to someone without the surrounding conversation.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice: "We will ...".

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. An ADR with no alternatives is usually a decision
that was never really made.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what now has to be true for it to keep working.
Include the costs — an ADR that only lists benefits is advertising, not a record.
